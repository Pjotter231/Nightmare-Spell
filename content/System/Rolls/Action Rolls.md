 This is a second example like in [[Rolling with Hope and Fear]] however going more through combining everything you've read so far.

In each scene, the GM and players go back and forth describing what happens. If you make a move where the outcome is in question, and the success or failure of that move is interesting to the story, your move is an action and the GM calls for an action roll to determine the outcome. However, if an action is either easy to pull off without complication or impossible to perform,  here’s no need to roll. You know the result already after all!

When the GM (or your spell, attack, or ability) asks you to make an action roll, you’ll do so by rolling the Duality Dice. Each action roll follows four basic steps, which are summarized here and detailed in the sections that follow:

## Step 1: Pick a character Stat
The GM will tell you which [[Stats|stat]] would be most applicable, however feel free to suggest a different one if you can justify it. Brutish intimidation with strength anyone? 

Let's assume we're trying to swim against a strong current. It will be challenging and we won't know for sure we'll succeed. Strength would be an appropriate roll here. Let's say we have +1 Strength.
## Step 2: Extra Bonuses
If we have an [[Experience]] that applies, let's say ["Bloodharbor Ripper"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cHRvLl8hpqc) on a check to swim. We can (and will in this example) spend a Hope to apply +2 to the roll. Experience is decided to add before making the roll, we can't say "oh 15, but I'm adding my experience just to be sure" or "oh I rolled with Hope I'm spending that on experience for this roll!"

In this example someone in our party can help us out too, but not enough to make it a free pass (don't ask how), and gives us advantage in the process. This means we get to add an extra 1d6 to our roll. 

Any other modifiers or dice, if allies have special Aspect Abilities to boost their allies these can also be applied here.
## Step 3: Lets roll!
We roll our 2d12 Duality dice. Hope 8 and 6 Fear for a 14 with Hope!
But we had advantage! Our extra 1d6 gave us another 3.
## Step 4: Math
Roll: 14 with **hope**
Advantage: 3
"Bloodharbor ripper" experience +2
Strength +1

Total: 14+3+2+1 = 20 with hope!

We succeed easily making it up the river for... reasons! And we even gained our hope we spend on experience back!

# CRITICAL HITS?!
Rolling twice the same number on your die results in a critical hit. Two 1's. Two 2's Two 3's etc. All count as a critical hit. This means that you automatically succeed, get what you want and a little extra. You, and everyone who witnesses you act gains a Hope. If you made an [[Attack Rolls|Attack Rolls]] you also deal extra damage. More on that in the [[Dealing some Damage!]] segment later. Or now if you click the link.
