Your character starts the game with 2 hope and your DM starts with 2 fear. When rolling dice in Nightmare Spell you roll 2 D12's. One hope and one fear D12. On top of these rolls can various modifiers, experiences, advantages or even hope be added. But I'll talk about those later. 

For now if you roll an 8 on your Hope die and a 3 on your Fear die you'll have rolled 'An 11 with Hope'. This means you gain a Hope token, which you can keep up to 5 and is kept between sessions. On rolling with Fear the GM gets a Fear token, they can keep up to 12 of these, are publicly visible and are kept between sessions. 

Note that only players can generate Hope and Fear.
### But what is Hope and Fear?
Hope can be used by players to start certain actions, help allies, use their [[Experience]] or remove negative [[Tarot Cards|Tarot]] effects.

Fear is used by the GM, they can activate special events, monster abilities/turns or otherwise turn up the heat using narrative tools. GM's also keep their fear in between sessions, however they can keep up to 12 and are publicly visible to everyone. Notice that your GM has been suddenly hoarding Fear? Better mentally prepare yourself for a truly harrowing fight.

### Examples
Lets say a player wants to sneak into a castle and climb the wall to find a window to enter from to do so.

The GM would set the climbing DC to succeed as 14 and can decide whether to share or keep this secret. The game master asks for an agility [[Action Rolls||action roll]] to which the player has a +1. They also utilize their expertise 'bad with boundaries' to gain an addition +2 modifier.
##### **Success with Hope**
A Hope 8 and Fear 5 is rolled +1 agility +2  experience for a total of 16 with Hope, as the Hope die was larger (>=) than Fear, and the total higher than the DC of 14 so a Success with Hope!

The player will manage to climb up the wall undetected, find an open window and climb in.
##### **Success with Fear**
A Hope 4 and Fear 9 is rolled +1 agility +2  experience for a total of 16 with Fear, as the Fear die was larger (>=) than Hope. And the total higher than the DC of 14, so a Success with Fear.

The player will manage to climb up the wall, however they might be detected as they do so, or find the window to be locked. Giving them the choice to either smash it or find another way in.
##### **Failure with Hope**
A Hope 5 and Fear 4 is rolled +1 agility +2  experience for a total of 12 with Hope, as the Fear die was larger (>=) than Hope. But the total lower than the DC of 14, so a Failure with Hope.

So the player would try and climb, but either straight up fail or fall down a short distance. However the noise they made remained minimal, and no guards with alerted.

##### **Failure with Fear**
A Hope 3 and Fear 6 is rolled +1 agility +2  experience for a total of 12 with Fear, as the Fear die was larger (>=) than Hope. But the total lower than the DC of 14, so a Failure with Fear.

A failure with hope is usually a true disaster. Maybe the character fell from a great height injuring themselves and alerting the guards, or they got detected mid climb and are now half way up a castle out in the open.

#### Note
Always keep in mind however that a success or failure isn't binary. A high roll will be a great success. Maybe players will find a clue that will lead them directly to the murderer with an 18. But a 12 would only give them a vague boot print.
