After landing an [[Attack Rolls]] you can roll for Damage!

Damage is usually dealt by using a characters [[Memories]] or [[Aspects & Flaws|Aspect Abilities]]. These can have the amount of damage they deal predetermined. A weapon Memory dealing d8 for example. Or they may be determined on the fly by improvised actions.

## Pre determined 
For predetermined damage you get to roll more dice the higher [[Awakened||rank]] your Memory is, or character if using an damaging Aspect ability.
The higher rank your character is the more dice they can roll. A Sleeper rolling with a [[Memories|memory]] that does 1d8+3 damage gets to roll a 1d8+3. However an Awakened gets to add a 2nd d8 and rolls 2d8+3.

Now of course most offensive Memories will have passive or active effects that also apply. If known these can ofcourse also be added on top.
