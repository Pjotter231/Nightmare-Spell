Damage is marked of as chunks. However incoming damage won't directly correlate to how many chunks to mark off.  [[Health|Mind, Body and Soul (MBS) chunks]] have the ability to reduce damage taken. 

You will most likely have some resistances per damage type. At least to body to start. These will grow as you get gear or increase your rank and or classification.

Below is an example damage table of a Sleeper wearing [[Ranks & Classification|dormant]] leather armour. A sleeper does not yet have a lot of gear to increase their mind & soul resistance. However their leather armour is giving them some physical resistance. 

|          | Minor 1 | Moderate  2 | Major 3 | Massive 4 | **Lethal** 6 |
| -------- | ------- | ----------- | ------- | --------- | ------------ |
| **Mind** | 1       | 2           | 4       | 8         | 16           |
| Body     | 2       | 4           | 8       | 12        | 16           |
| **Soul** | 1       | 1           | 2       | 2         | 4            |

Body: Minor 2 - Moderate 4 - Major 6 - Massive 12 - Lethal 20

The way you 'calculate' damage is taking the incoming damage and looking up where it falls on the table. Using the rule 'meets it beats it'. So for example our Sleeper taking 4 body damage would have them mark of 2 body chunks. Up until 7 damage this would remain as 2 chunks, where it would become 3 chunks at 8 damage. However should they take 1 damage (less than 2 for minor) your character will completely shrug it off (or evade if you'd rather have the story about your character told like that)
### Upgrading resistances
As briefly mentioned before gear can increase your resistances. Armour, amulets, rings, aspect abilities and more can all increase or even decrease your resistances to these 3 damage types.
### What about Fate chunks?
Fate damage is taken when your character receives chunk damage to an MBS bar that's already depleted (or over kill damage is done). Damage taken will still go according to the corresponding MBS bar's damage table but will simply apply to the characters Fate chunks.
### What about specific resistances?
Specific resistances, such as fire resistance halves incoming damage before looking at the damage table.
# "No it didn't"
Once a famous quote, now a game mechanic. Got hit bad? Wish it hadn't? Or at least less bad? 

Spend a Hope!

You can spend one hope to reduce the incoming Damage tier by one once per hit. Took a Major hit with only 3 chunks left? Reduce it to Moderate by spending a Hope. Note that you cannot choose to spend more Hope to reduce more Damage.
