# "I pull out my glock19 and kill the Monster dead"
Not so fast fucko.

When I say 'whatever they want' I mean that there is no turn-order. There are no 'actions, bonus actions, reaction actions, bonus bonus actions'. There are just Moves™.

A move is every single thing you do within your 'turn'. Lets call them Moments.

### Moment(s) of Silence for Bonus Actions
Whenever someone else's Moment™ ends and they're satisfied with their Move™. You can decide you want your own Moment™ and tell the table (or discord call) your Move™. 

A Move encompasses your entire Moment. Moving to your injured ally and healing them is a move. Climbing a mast and jumping into the sea from high is a move. Keeping up the pressure and attacking an enemy is a move.

If you still really have a hard time imagining what a Move™ is just think about everything you'd do in your normal TTRPG like Dungeons and Dragons. You'd move somewhere and attack something. You'd jump of a ledge and ambush something below. You'd pull a lever to drain the acid from a room.
- You can still do all of these things, but without worrying about the exact distance you'd have to walk.

You'd also look at your actions and bonus actions. And can I still offhand attack after this? How many feet did I move again? Ah shit I already used my bonus action now I can't heal! 
- You now no longer have to worry about 'what type of action is this?'. Because its a Move™.

You'd do some really cool acrobatics and stab a bandit chief in the skull! Aaaand then throw a dagger at a random thief nearby because fuck it, I have an action left. Deluting your initial intend to satisfy the inner minmaxxer.
- You no longer 'have to' get the optimal useage out of all your actions. You can just do what you want to do.
## Rule of Thumb
Generally one attack per arm or able limb is a safe bet plus some movement and whatever else is appropriate. Though if you think, and the rest of the table agrees, that more is fitting then by all means go for it! 

## With great power...
Now this puts a LOT of agency in the hands of players. And this means that everyone at the table or discord call, will have to be responsible with it. Take care not to hog the spotlight, let others have their Moments™. Or have them together!
