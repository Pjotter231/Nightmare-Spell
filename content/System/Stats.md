Player characters have six main stats. They start of with +2 +1 +0 -1 -2 to four of these stats. The six main stats are:
##### **Agility (Sprint, Parkour)**
High agility makes you faster, more mobile, quicker to react to danger. Used during acrobatic actions, jumping behind cover or dodging a trap.
##### **Strength (Smashing, Lifting)**
High strength helps you be more destructive, brace against impacts, last longer in a battle of endurance. Used to smash doors, throw small objects or creatures.
##### **Finesse (Sleight of Hands, Stealth)**
High finesse helps your fine motor control. Making it easier to pick both locks and pockets, or avoiding twigs to move silently. As well as artifice with delicate mechanics. Used to remain unnoticed, use fine tools or act with otherwise high precision. 
##### **Instinct (Perceive, Sense, Track)**
High instinct helps you notice danger quicker either via increased perception or a 6th sense. As well as track or otherwise navigate around. Used to notice danger, traps, tracks and to remain on the right path.
##### **Presence (Deceive, Convince)**
High presence helps you exert influence over others by either convincing, intimidating, deceive or otherwise affect those around you. 
##### **Knowledge (Recall, Deduct, Arcane)**
High knowledge allows you to recall more about previously obtained information, environments. But also allows you to better understand matters through deduction or pattern recognition. As well as helping you understand and use magic in the form of runes, weave or otherwise. Used when remembering a familiar symbol, understanding a clue or filling in the gaps in some mystery or using arcane runes to fly a ship.
## When don't they matter
 What makes Nightmare Spell different from more traditional TTRPGs is that this is a roleplay game first. If your character would normally be able to lockpick a simple lock because they've gotten good enough at it over the ranks no roll might be required. Or if one has sufficient proof of an affair, one might not even need to roll intimidation to blackmail them. Or perhaps you've used specific runes already. The DM won't call for a roll in these cases and simply let your character do it.
# Essence
Besides the four health bars players have a Essence bar. This one starts at 5, which grows with [[Ranks & Classification]]. Essence is spend by using aspect abilities or doing an exhausting action that requires additional essence. Essence can also be spend to increase their roll or evasion.

One can see it as their mana, except for that this energy can also enhance their body.
