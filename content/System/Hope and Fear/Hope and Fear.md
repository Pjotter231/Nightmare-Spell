Where [[Rolling with Hope and Fear]] focuses on the number side of things and is more of an introduction on what Hope and Fear is and how to get it. This chapter will focus on what you and your GM can actually spend it on as well as listing every way of gaining it without the number bloat.

Hope and Fear can only be generated by players. So whilst GM's can spend their fear, they can't roll a critical hit, bash your skull in AND generate a Fear for themselves. They can ONLY bash your skull in.

Both Hope and Fear are saved in between sessions and do not reset. A game starts with the players have two Fear, and the GM starts with one Fear per Player joining the Campaign/One shot/Module
# Hope
"To live without Hope is to cease to live"

Hope separate per character, each character can hold up to five Hope at a time. Any Hope gained above this number is wasted.
## Spending Hope
You may have seen some mentions of spending Hope already depending on the order in which you read things, but here is the (hopefully) complete list of everything you can spend a hope on.

**Helping others** (2)
When helping others, and this doesn't make it an immediate success, you can spend one Hope to give other players advantage (+1d6).

**Experience** (1)
Maybe more exciting them helping others (ew), you can spend one Hope to use an [[Experience]]. 

**Reducing Damage** (2)
Following the "No it didn't" rule, you can spend two Hope to [[Damage Reduction|reduce]] the incoming chunk damage by 1.
## Gaining Hope
Now we know how to spend it, how do we get Hope?

- [[Rolling with Hope and Fear|Rolling with Hope]], your hope die was larger than your fear die so regardless of result you gain a Hope.
- Critical success, succeeding with a critical roll gives you one Hope
	- Witnessing a critical success also generates one Hope
- Doing good RP, when being especially awesome (and your GM remembers this rule) you gain a Hope.
- Witnessing a turning point from a desperate situation to a hopeful one, for what is Hope if not the belief that things will end well? 
- Being on time, being on time for the session gives you a Hope
# Fear
"Fear nothing, but the GM with fourteen"

Gamemasters can gain up to twelve fear, and can spend it in a few ways. Unlike players, who each have their individual Hope pool.
## Spending Fear
The game master can Spend fear in a few ways, which will usually be the following but might have some more creative applications too. Fear is meant to keep the game exciting and fluid by spicing scenes and fights up a little. 

**Making extra GM Moves** (1)
Making additional GM moves, outside of normal reactive GM moves fear my be spend to gain extra Moves

**Fear Features** (varies)
Using special Fear features of enemies or terrain, these can be especially powerful or fight turning abilities

**Adding to Player Moves** (varies)
Adding on top of a players Move, or forcing them to rephrase their action by introducing an additional obstacle. This obstacle may not be immediately obvious

**For Fun** (varies)
Just to see how the players react thinking something bad is happening now you know, because there totally isn't anything bad going to happen, right? 
## Gaining Fear
Now that you know what the GM can spend Fear on, let's go over how they can gain Fear. 

First of all, the GM cannot generate any fear themselves. Only players can generate Fear and there are two ways of doing this.
- [[Rolling with Hope and Fear|Rolling with Fear]], your Fear die was larger than your Hope gives the GM a Fear
- Being too late, showing up after the agreed upon time generates a point of Fear
