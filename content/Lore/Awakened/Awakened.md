Awakened are what all humans who have survived the Winter Solstice are called. Not to be confused with the actual [[Ranks & Classification|rank]] Awakened. A Master or Saint are still called awakened when spoken about as a group. Sleepers are usually not included when referring to awakened.

Awakened come in seven different ranks. As mentioned in [[Ranks & Classification]].


| Rank        | Human Name | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |

| Dormant     | Sleeper    | Survivors of the First Nightmare. They have gained awareness of their Essence, access to their [[Soul Core & Sea]], ability to gain and summon [[Memories]] and [[Echoes]] aswell as the ability to become stronger by absorbing [[Soul Shards]].<br>The next Winter Solstice will drag them back to the [[Dream Realm]] until they find a Citadel to return to the Waking Realm, also known as Earth. |

They have unlocked their second Aspect ability.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

## Ranking up
To rank up one must challenge a [[Nightmare]] by taking on either a [[Nightmare Gate]] or [[Nightmare Seed]].
