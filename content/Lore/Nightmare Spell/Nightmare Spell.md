The Nightmare spell appeared just as Earth had barely started to stabilize. People started to fall into an unnatural slumber. Some woke up wielding supernatural strength and abilities, whilst others never woke and instead birthed monsters who crawled out of their corpses. Monsters quickly dubbed Nightmare creatures, who were oddly resistant to mundane forms of attack not originating from weapons or powers from those who had awoken.

Those that did awake came back from stories of strange lands where they had to survive often horrific trials in ancient civilizations to prove their worth and received a rating from an entity or system called [[The Spell]]. They gained an [[Aspects & Flaws|Aspect & Flaw]] as well as a [[Soul Core & Sea]]. Some Sleepers also gain a [[True Name]], this name embodies their very being. It is often able to be interpreted in different ways depending on how the runes are read.
Their Aspect allowing them to use magical abilities and their soul core strengthening them to almost super human strength. An average person would immediately be lifted to the strength and endurance of a multi discipline Olympic athlete even as a fresh Nightmare survivor.

Sleepers was the term coined for those people who awoke from their Nightmare. 

## Winter Solstice
During the first Winter Solstice every Sleeper once again fell asleep, once more transported to a strange land. However different, no longer did they find themselves in an ancient civilization surrounded by other people. Instead they found themselves in ancient forest, ruins and deadly environments. They could find other Sleepers, however they could not find anyone not from Earth.

They spend ages trying to find out how to return to earth. Until they discovered what were later named Citadels. Often Ruins of Fortresses with an Alter or other object that allowed Sleepers to attune to them and return to their waking world's body.

Now every awakened that falls asleep will wake up in the other realm, in the citadel they attuned themselves to. Some would simply sleep and return when waking up, others would explore this dream realm further to gain strength in the form of [[Soul Shards]], [[Memories]] and [[Echoes]].
