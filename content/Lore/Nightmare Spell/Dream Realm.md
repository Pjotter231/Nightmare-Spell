The realm where Sleepers are thrust to during the winter solstice, as well as where Awakened go every time they fall asleep. It is a vast land, dangerous and mostly unexplored land. Vast regions with different laws stretch across it. Freezing mountains, ashen forests, toxic swamps, raging oceans. All of them hostile an dangerous. Not only do Nightmare Creatures lurk everywhere, often the very land is dangerous or mystical forces introduce new laws of magical nature.

A few [[Citadels]] have been established, some larger than others. Yet still so many are yet to be found. Either hidden away or unreachable deep inside Death Zones.

### Death zones
All zones in the Dream Realm are hostile, Nightmare creatures lurking everywhere. However few areas are more dangerous still. Anyone entering those are rarely seen return, and only those truly strong do. 

Any Sleeper who find themselves in a place not known to mankind on their Winter Solstice can safely bet on being in either a death zone itself, or in a place unreachable hidden behind one.

## Dream Realm vs Waking World
The Dream Realm is a whole separate realm and equally as real as the waking world. And travel between it isn't fully understood yet. Though as far as scientists are aware Sleepers and Awakened make their consciousness travel between Realms whilst 'sharing' the soul. And Ascended and Saint's merge these two bodies. 

Instances have occurred where masters will regain their bodies upon Ascending from Awakened after having one or both destroyed. Though those are rare or at least the subsequent survival of whatever killed their body last time is. It is hard to be accurate with limited data.

### Death in both Realms
Awakened that die in a Nightmare turn into Nightmare Creatures. Any awakened whose conscious body dies in either realm turns their body in the other realm Comatose, also referred to as Hollows. 

However curiously, when the unconscious body dies the conscious body simply loses the ability to return to the other realm, referred to creatively as Reverse Hollows. Until they become ascended and gain the ability to physically travel between realms.
