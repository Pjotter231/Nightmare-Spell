The realm where Sleepers are thrust to during the winter solstice, as well as where Awakened go every time they fall asleep. It is a vast land, dangerous and mostly unexplored land. Vast regions with different laws stretch across it. Freezing mountains, ashen forests, toxic swamps, raging oceans. All of them hostile an dangerous. Not only do Nightmare Creatures lurk everywhere, often the very land is dangerous or mystical forces introduce new laws of magical nature.

A few [[Citadels]] have been established, some larger than others. Yet still so many are yet to be found. Either hidden away or unreachable deep inside Death Zones.

## Deathzones
All zones in the Dream Realm are hostile.
