Nightmares are special trials designed for [[Awakened]]. Whilst each rank of Nightmare has a slight variation to it most follow common lines.

## The First Nightmare
Briefly, the first Nightmare is a place where Sleepers are challenged to survive and try and influence the past. The greater the impact they had the greater they are rewarded with a stronger and higher rank Aspect. More on specifically the [[The First Nightmare||first nightmare here]].

## Winter Solstice vs Nightmare
The Winter Solstice sends Sleepers to the actual Dream Realm that they can find other Awakened in. It is the present state of the Realm. Nightmares however always take place in the past. The First Nightmare will always be challenged alone. Whilst later ones can be challenged as a group. More on that in the chapter below.

# Challenging Nightmares
Besides the first nightmare, all other Nightmares can be challenged together. This is done by either falling asleep near a [[Nightmare Gate]] or challenging a [[Nightmare Seed]].

Both Gates and Seeds can be challenged together by being pulled into them at the same time.
