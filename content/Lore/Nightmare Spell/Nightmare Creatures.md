Nightmare creatures come in varying shapes forms and [[Ranks & Classification|ranks]]. They are corrupted beings whoes only goal is to destroy and kill or corrupt all beings that are not corrupted.

### Creature ranks

| Rank    | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Beast   | Beasts are simple creatures, often no more than a wild aggressive animal albeit with potentially huge amounts of strength but otherwise mundane.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Monster | Monsters are like a beast but more cunning and savage.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Demon   | Demons have no longer have a simple mind and might employ rudimentary strategies.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Devil   | Devils go a step further an approach human intelligence albeit corrupted and used in a purely destructive manner. Furthermore these beings might start manifesting otherworldly magical abilities.                                                                                                                                                     |
| Tyrant  | Tyrants can control large swarms of minions. Often themselves not much stronger than Devils individually, their strength comes from the amount of minions they surround themselves with.                                                                                                                                                               |
| Terror  | Terrors take this a step further an often try to control large swaths of area. Their control over others might also no longer be limited to just their minions.<br>They are quite diverse and this control might range from being able to bend terrain to their liking on large scales or try and attempt to forcefully control creatures around them. |
| Titan   | Titans are an exception to the rule where previous classes take previous ones a step further. These often no longer surround themselves by minions under their control, and are simply power itself. <br>Weaker nightmare creatures might still flock to it however.                                                                                   |
