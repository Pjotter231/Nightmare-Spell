Gates that appear in the Waking World where Nightmare creatures poor out. Every gate usually consists of a few waves of Nightmare Creatures pouring out following by one or a few stronger Nightmare Creatures referred to as Gate Guardians.

Should the gate not be closed nightmare creatures will keep pouring out at a reduced rate after the initial breach.

## Call of the Gate
A gate exudes a constant pull on all Awakened nearby. As if it calls for them. Awakened have to resist it to not fall asleep near it. Falling asleep near a gate of equal or higher tier pulls you into it and forces you to challenge a [[Nightmare]] of the Gates tier. A tier 2 gate for a 2nd Nightmare.

For example awakened can challenge gates of tier 2 and higher. Whilst Ascended can challenge tier 3 and higher.

Tier 2 gates often have Fallen Gate Guardians, Tier 3 usually contains a Corrupted class Nightmare Creature. Tier 4, Great. A tier 5 gate in America had a Cursed Nightmare Creature appear, and resulted in the fall of the Continent. 
## Closing a Gate
To close a gate an Awakened of lower rank must stop resisting the pull and get thrown into the nightmare. 

Beating the Nightmare results in closing the gate as well as the usual rewards for beating a Nightmare. 

Awakened can work together beating Gates by falling asleep simultaneously, they will have to find each other still within the Nightmare but they can work together to beat it.
