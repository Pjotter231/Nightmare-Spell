### Good old Days
Centuries ago there were twelve billion people living on earth, with a few million on the moon, however now barely three billion remain.

Earth was at war for dwindling resources during the climate collapse. Cities were drowning after the sea level rose from both poles completely melting, other natural disasters wiped out infrastructure needed to sustain large population.
The sea level had risen so much that billions of people had to relocate, despite only a relatively small portion of land being drowned many cities were located near the oceans. Not enough resources could be gathered with the infrastructure failing to house and feed all these refugees. People died of starvation and poor living conditions in droves. 

And so, in a very human fashion, a war started for resources. Weapons of mass destruction were deployed en masse, nuclear-, chemical-, bio-warfare nothing was left untouched. Kinetic bombardments turning entire areas to molten slag. So much was unleashed in Europe that rather than the war ending, Europe simply became so uninhabitable nothing left of value to wage war over was left. Only a toxic desolate nuclear wasteland.

And just as the planet was recovering from all those disasters, both man made and natural the [[Nightmare Spell]] appeared.
