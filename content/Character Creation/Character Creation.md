# Step 1: Creation
The first step to becoming an [[Awakened]] (or even Sleeper) is to be a Human first and so to create one is the first step. 

## 'Just' a Human
### Basics: ASL
Where did they live? What did they do before getting infected by the [[Nightmare Spell]]? Do they have a family? A job? Did they live in a now abandoned region?  Do they now live in a huge siege capital or one of the smaller cities? Or perhaps are they one of the rare people living rurally still? Do they even have a house? Are they starving or well of?

### To be Human
Humans have these silly things called emotions and feelings. They have dreams, worries and the like. Whilst these are less important to roughly shape your character it can help you get more into playing them. 

Did they want to become an Awakened? What would they want to do once (and if) they beat their first Nightmare?

### Silly details
What's their morning routine like? What does their beroom (if they have one) look like? All the questions about your character that you do not need to write down on the main "about" area of your character, or even worry about writing about at all! 

However I find that thinking about quirky things of characters allows me to get even more into them and even find out quirky things about them that I hadn't considered before but make perfect sense.

They like coffee with lots of sugar? Why? 
Because they never got to spoil themselves earlier in their lives. 

Boom, more insight into your own character, a fun detail, or perhaps a whole new direction that you could go with. Really this mini step is very very optional but its a fun exercise. 

### Getting Infected
What did they do once they realized they were infected? Did they panic? Find a police station to follow standard protocol? Did they try and prepare for their [[The First Nightmare|First Nightmare]]? If so what did they do? How did their family react? How old were they when they attracted the Nightmare Spell? How far way is the next

## Writing
When writing your character feel free to answer whatever questions I just asked. They are here simply to guide you and get you thinking about it.

# Step 2: The First Nightmare
As you can read [[The First Nightmare|here about the First Nightmare]] Sleepers and Awakened are taken to a distant past of the [[Dream Realm]]. Where they have to overcome a challenge of some sorts. To take part in event that has already occurred.

### Your very own Nightmare!
The GM after reading your human character will either start writing, adjust an already written one, whatever they do they'll start to cook up your very own Nightmare. Once it is done they'll try and schedule a session with you. A one on one session. Just you, your GM and whatever evil Nightmare they might have cooked up.

Having beaten the first Nightmare you are greeted with your appraisal. This is the list of your accomplishments from your Nightmare. It will mention any notable actions you've taken, any notable figures saved or slain. And finally a rating and Aspect Rank. With all of this on to step 3 you go!
# Step 3: Creation (again!)
You can start this either immediately after finishing the session (or perhaps even before though it can be fun to use your own actions in the First Nightmare as inspiration). Or you can discuss it later, either through text or in a call at a later date. 

Point is you get to talk with your GM about what kind of [[Aspects & Flaws|Aspect & Flaw]] you'd like and it's 1st (dormant) ability. Keeping in mind that you will be 'merely' a Sleeper and will not possess demi-god like abilities just yet. 

If unsure please take a look at some [[Example Sleepers]]! Hopefully this gives you some inspiration. Just remember aspect abilities can be as direct or indirect as you want. "Shoot firebolt" (bolt not ball) is a totally valid dormant ability. Though rephrasing it into 'Create fire' or maybe 'Fire Control' will be better to allow for more creative future growth.

# Step 4+: Let the games Begin
Future growth begins now!

Earth has a special education system for Sleepers who have beaten their first Nightmare. The goal is to teach them as much as possible about the Dream Realm before the next [[Nightmare Spell|Winter Solstice]] giving them the best ability to survive. 

This means there might be an Academy Arc where everyone will be able to learn about each other characters together, really like mentioned in the caption this is where the game already begins. 

I've marked this as its own step despite really already counting as game start, as every time you reach a new rank, or the first time you gain a core you'll need to co-operatively decide on how this would increase, improve or slightly change your abilities. 

Does your flaw grow stronger as your rank does? What would happen if you gain a core, can you now control 2 shadows? Did gaining a rank allow you to now also empower others and not just yourself? What about your totally new aspect ability you gained upon ranking up. Or your transformation ability you got as a saint.
